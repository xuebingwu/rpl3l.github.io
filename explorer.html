How to read this table — and how not to

AlphaMissense predicts general missense deleteriousness, not CMD2D. The disease mechanism is specific: recurrent hotspot variants are toxic because they combine loss of function with preservation of RPL3L's repression of RPL3 — a property no sequence-based predictor captures. Note that several confirmed CMD2D alleles (e.g., p.Thr189Met, p.Arg58Gln, p.Glu202Gly) are predicted benign — exactly as the dual loss-/gain-of-function model predicts for non-hotspot alleles, which lose RPL3L function without blocking RPL3 compensation. A "likely pathogenic" prediction also does not mean a variant causes CMD2D: biallelic inheritance and rarity are required too. The most useful shortlist is high AM score + absent from gnomAD (use the frequency filter below). Predictions are a research aid, not clinical evidence — see the curated allele table for experimentally characterized variants.
