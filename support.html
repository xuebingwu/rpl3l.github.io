Propose a collaboration

Donors

Ultra-rare diseases like CMD2D rarely attract large-scale funding — with only a handful of known families, philanthropy is often what moves research from discovery toward treatment. Your gift helps us:

  • Build the patient registry so every affected family can be found, connected, and counted
  • Characterize variants so families get clearer genetic answers, faster
  • Develop disease models — patient stem-cell–derived heart cells and engineered heart tissue
  • Explore therapies that exploit the RPL3–RPL3L paralog system, including RPL3, the heart's back-up ribosomal protein

Gifts can be made in honor or memory of a loved one, and we welcome partnerships with foundations and companies. Contact us and we will share how to give and how your support will be used.
