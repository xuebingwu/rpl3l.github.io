Ways to get involved

Families

Join the registry, share your story, connect with other CMD2D families. Your experience is data — and hope.

Clinicians

Refer suspected or confirmed cases; contribute de-identified clinical data; help us build recognition tools for neonatal DCM workups.

Researchers

Propose collaborations and request cell lines and reagents as they become available.

Contact

The RPL3L Initiative is in formation. Whether you are a family, clinician, or researcher, please reach out:

rpl3l-initiative@cumc.columbia.edu
